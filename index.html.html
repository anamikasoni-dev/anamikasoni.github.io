03

RAKSHAK: Intelligent Multi-Hazard Early Warning System

Disaster Management · Hardware · Problem Statement 26178 · Team The Rebellion

An edge-intelligent, fault-aware network of solar-powered sensor nodes that turns hyperlocal environmental readings into resilient, actionable alerts for floods, forest fires, air pollution, extreme heat, landslides, industrial hazards and water quality.

  • Edge-first: each ESP32 node does calibration, filtering, sensor-health checks and feature extraction locally, then computes a 0–100 risk score.
  • Cross-node verification: weighted scoring, hazard-specific logic and temporal trends across neighbouring nodes cut false alarms.
  • Resilient comms: LoRa/LoRaWAN for long range, Wi-Fi where available and GSM/SMS as backup.
  • Clear output: SAFE / WATCH / DANGER / SENSOR FAULT levels shown on a live GIS dashboard (Firebase/MQTT), local OLED, buzzer and LEDs, with authority and community alerts.
  • Built a working hardware prototype and web dashboard.

04

Helmet-Mounted Conformal Antenna for Tactical Communications

Robotics & Drones · Hardware · Problem Statement 26185 · Team The Tech Hunters

A zero-profile, self-selecting antenna system for urban close-quarter battle, designed to remove snagging hazards and signal loss from protruding whip antennas.

  • Four flexible conformal antennas on the helmet give spatial diversity against body blockage and changing orientation.
  • Link-quality-based switching: an ESP32 monitors packet/ACK success over an HC-12 (433 MHz) link and drives an SP4T RF switch to the best antenna, with no manual intervention.
  • MPU6050 IMU assists selection by sensing helmet orientation, and a conductive reflector layer directs radiation outward and reduces head-side exposure.
  • ESP32-S3 camera for video; rugged coaxial connection to existing radios.
  • Covered feasibility, financials, risks and mitigation, and a research base on helmet conformal arrays.

05

Ferroscan: Autonomous Multi-Sensor Mineral Prospectivity Mapping

Geospatial AI · Mining

An AI platform that combines drone imagery, geophysical sensor data and geological datasets into a GIS prospectivity map to guide targeted drilling, replacing weeks of manual sampling and speculative drilling.

  • Drone payloads: RGB, multispectral/SWIR, LiDAR and magnetometer, plus DEM/SRTM terrain and GPS/IMU ground validation.
  • OpenDroneMap pipeline for orthomosaics and georeferencing; iron-oxide and clay index extraction.
  • XGBoost and Random Forest fuse spectral, magnetic and elevation data; edge AI runs onboard for low-connectivity sites.
  • Dashboard with probability and risk zones, shortfall prediction, recommendations and lease-level reports.
  • Planned for DGCA/DGMS compliance, false-positive anomalies and sensor calibration.

06

AETHER AI: TinyML Machine Health Monitoring

TinyML · Predictive maintenance

A low-cost edge system that classifies a running motor as healthy or faulty in real time, aimed at small workshops and educational labs.

  • MPU-6500 IMU for vibration signatures and ACS712 current sensor for load profile, read by a NodeMCU.
  • Model trained and deployed through Edge Impulse on the Motor Vibration Classification IMU dataset, running on-device.
  • Status on an OLED with a buzzer alert; live web dashboard with confidence (99.61% in the recorded healthy run), accelerometer/gyro sparklines, current and vibration graphs.
  • Next: multi-class faults (bearing wear, imbalance, misalignment) and cloud logging.
